Active Solar Tracker data link

Every minute the tracker logs the power of the tracked panel, the fixed panel and its own electronics. Every 15 minutes it uploads to ThingSpeak, every night MATLAB summarises each day, and the phone dashboard shows all of it live.

How it works

  1. MeasureThree power sensors (INA226 or INA219, found automatically): tracked panel, fixed panel, tracker's own use on the 5 V feed. Angles and state from the control code. Once a second.
  2. LogArduino Nano ESP32 averages each UTC minute and keeps 8 days in flash, so Wi-Fi drops lose nothing.
  3. UploadHTTPS bulk upload to ThingSpeak channel 3578277 every 15 min.
  4. AnalyseMATLAB on ThingSpeak, 00:20 UTC nightly: energy, gross and net gain, pointing error, completeness into channel 3578278.
  5. ShowPhone dashboard: live figures, charts, daily and per-run tables, CSV export, and a MATLAB check on every day.

What the headline result depends on

In November, tracking gains about as much energy as the electronics use. PVGIS for Guildford with the 5.4 W panel: an inclined-axis tracker beats a fixed panel at the same tilt by about 1.5 Wh a day; a flat tracker beats a flat fixed panel by about 2.5 Wh. The firmware as built (board awake, radio off between uploads, servo switched off between moves) is modelled at about 2.9 Wh a day, and an ESP32 left awake uses about 4.8. A servo left powered would add 1.2–4.8 Wh a day on its own, which is why it is switched off between moves.

Decided 17 Nov (Lem): P_use is everything the tracker draws (MCU with Wi-Fi, sensors, servo). Over a year the PVGIS model gives +29 % gross and +14 % net of the data link, but a net loss in the winter months while the board stays awake, so the lab runs will probably show a loss. Report gross and net month by month: figure and table. Light sleep between samples would keep every month positive.

Still to confirm before the first outdoor run: the fixed reference panel sits at the tracker's tilt.
