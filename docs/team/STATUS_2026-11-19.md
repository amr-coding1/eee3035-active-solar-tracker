4. **The servo may not hold the panel when unpowered.** It has plastic gears and a slip clutch, so test this first (M9). If it drifts, we need a brake, a balanced mount, or to accept the standby current.
5. **Energy: the board awake loses in winter.** It is modelled at 2.87 Wh/day; light sleep would bring it to about 0.49 Wh/day. Measuring P_use on the bench is the evidence the marker asked for.
6. **The dashboard now matches the servo.** Field 7 is V_supply, the 5 V rail; it stays in the CSV only. Faults 7/8 and the MOV state use servo wording, and the contract and energy model are updated.

## What was built on 19 Nov (AM, with AI, logged)

`transmission.cpp` is done, and it sits inside a complete tracker firmware: [`firmware/tracker`](../../firmware/tracker/README.md). It opens in the Arduino IDE (`tracker.ino`) and in VS Code with PlatformIO (`firmware/platformio.ini`). All the files compile together into one program. The data link runs in its own background tasks, and `loop()` runs the control cycle five times a second, so the parts work at the same time.

| File | Does | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `main.cpp` | `setup()` starts each module; `loop()` runs control at 5 Hz, the servo sequence and the serial port | AM, with CO |
| `transmission.h/.cpp` | Sends the data. Reads the 3 power sensors (INA226 or INA219, detected automatically) and keeps 1-min records in flash for 8 days. Uploads to ThingSpeak every 15 min over HTTPS. Handles serial commands. Others call only `transmission::report()` | AM |
| `sensing.h/.cpp` | Four BPX65 readings to a light direction (cosine fit), with `sun cal dark` / `sun cal flat` calibration | CO (starter code) |
| `servo.h/.cpp` | DS-R001 on D9, power switch on D8. Each move is power on, ramp at 30°/s, settle, power off. Remembers the last angle | CH (starter code) |
| `control.h/.cpp` | Re-points when more than 5° off, within ±45°. Stows at −45° after 1 min dark, holds when the light has no direction. Raises faults 5/7/8, with stall protection from P_use. Has `servo test` for the inclinometer check and `live on` | CO (starter code) |
| `config.h` | Every pin and setting in one place | all |

## What we missed (most important first)

1. **The hardware design is not frozen.** The diagram and the BOM disagree (table above), and "Servo.h started" was minuted with no file in the repo. Decide the bench set-up by Mon 23 Nov, or Wednesday tests the wrong thing.
2. **The servo's standby current and holding.** Switching it off saves 1.2–4.8 Wh/day but only works if it holds unpowered (M9). This is a real trade-off for the report, and a decision matrix (servo vs worm gear) is now expected because we changed actuator.
3. **The power bank may switch itself off.** Many banks cut out below 50–100 mA. A sleeping tracker draws about 3 mA (M13). The BOM's Waveshare manager + 18650 does not have this problem.
4. **P_use must cover everything.** If the Nano and the servo come off the bank as two branches, both need a sensor (`kInaUse2`), or measure before the split. Otherwise the net gain is too optimistic.
5. **Photodiodes at 3.3 V.** The 12 Nov arc test ran on a 5 V Uno. No Nano ESP32 pin takes 5 V, so redo the load resistors for 3V3 and keep every channel below about 3.0 V under the lamp.
6. **No light sleep yet.** With the board awake the tracker loses energy in winter, which is the headline result. It is the next firmware change after the bench numbers (M2, M4).
7. **No clock in the diagram.** Without the DS3231 the data is timestamped only after NTP, and every sample carries fault 4.
8. **The data is public, but the DMP and ethics form say otherwise.** The ThingSpeak channels and the repo are public; the submitted forms say the data stays private. Still to agree and minute.
9. **AI log.** Vlad will check it strictly (minutes, 19 Nov). Most of our AI use is still unlogged.
10. **Project management.** The brief scored 11/20 here. The Gantt needs work packages and a critical path, and the risk register needs the new risks (servo, power bank, design change).
